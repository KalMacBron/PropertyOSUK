Built by a UK landlord

Created from the work behind the portfolio.

PropertyOS began with a familiar problem: property information spread across spreadsheets, calendar reminders, email, documents and memory.

As a portfolio grows, the risk is not usually a lack of effort. It is that the next important date, repair, task or document is hidden in the wrong place.

PropertyOS is being built as the workspace its founder wanted to use: calm, traceable and focused on the practical work of managing UK residential property.

The private Alpha is now being tested with real landlord journeys. The founding programme will open after that feedback has been assessed.

Who it is for

Self-managing UK landlords with growing portfolios.

You may be a good fit if…

  • You manage UK residential property yourself.
  • You have outgrown spreadsheets and scattered reminders.
  • You want property records and operational work in one place.
  • You value a clear audit trail and exportable data.

It is not currently designed for…

  • Letting agencies managing property for multiple clients.
  • Commercial property or short-let operations.
  • Replacing legal, tax, accounting or compliance advice.
  • Landlords operating outside the UK.

Data, access and security

Clear boundaries, without claims ahead of the evidence.

Organisation-scoped records

Business records are linked to an organisation and protected by database row-level security designed to prevent cross-organisation access.

Private documents

Uploaded evidence is stored privately and accessed through authorised links rather than public file URLs.

Roles and read-only access

Owner, Admin, Member and Viewer roles support different capabilities. Viewer write access is blocked in both the interface and database policies.

Portfolio export

Owners and Admins can download a formatted Excel portfolio report. Bulk document ZIP export is not currently provided.

Private Alpha status: email confirmation is enabled. Multi-factor authentication, formal backup/restore evidence and wider security acceptance testing remain planned work and are not being advertised as completed.
